Old Gold Calculator India

Calculate the estimated value of your old gold based on its weight, purity and current gold rate in India.

How to Calculate Old Gold Value?

Old Gold Value = Weight × Rate Based on Gold Purity

Old Gold Purity

Old jewellery can have different levels of purity such as 24K, 22K, 18K and 14K. The purity should be checked before calculating its estimated value.

How Old Gold Value is Calculated

First determine the weight and purity of the gold. Then apply the appropriate gold rate to estimate the value. A jeweller may also deduct applicable charges or melting and refining costs when buying old gold.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is old gold value calculated?

Old gold value depends mainly on its weight, purity and the applicable gold rate.

Does old jewellery have making charges?

When selling old jewellery, the original making charges are generally not added to its gold value. The buyer may apply deductions according to their policies.
