How to Calculate 18K Gold Value?

Gold Value = Weight × 18K Gold Rate per Gram

What is 18K Gold?

18K gold contains approximately 75% pure gold. The remaining portion consists of other metals that can improve the strength and durability of jewellery.

18K Gold Purity

18K gold has a fineness of approximately 750, meaning it contains about 750 parts pure gold out of 1000.

18K Gold Jewellery Price

The final jewellery price may include making charges, GST, wastage and other applicable charges. These are separate from the basic gold value.
