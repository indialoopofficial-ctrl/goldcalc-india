Gold Purity and Karat

Gold purity is commonly expressed in karats (K) or fineness. Pure gold is generally considered 24K.

Common Gold Purity Values

  • 24K: Approximately 99.9% pure gold
  • 22K: Approximately 91.6% pure gold
  • 18K: Approximately 75% pure gold
  • 14K: Approximately 58.5% pure gold

How to Calculate Gold Purity?

A simple formula is:

Gold Purity % = Karat ÷ 24 × 100

For example, 22K gold is approximately:

22 ÷ 24 × 100 = 91.67%

Why Gold Purity Matters

Gold purity affects the value of jewellery and other gold items. Higher purity generally means a higher proportion of pure gold in the item.
