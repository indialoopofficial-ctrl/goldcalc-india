What is 22K Gold?

22K gold contains approximately 91.6% pure gold. The remaining portion is made up of other metals that help make jewellery stronger and more suitable for everyday use.

How to Calculate 22K Gold Value

The basic estimated gold value can be calculated from the jewellery weight, applicable gold rate and 22K purity.

For example, if you have 10 grams of 22K gold, the estimated gold value depends on the applicable price per gram. Jewellery making charges, wastage and GST may be added separately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 22K gold purity?

22K gold is approximately 91.6% pure gold, commonly represented as 916 fineness.

Is 22K gold suitable for jewellery?

Yes. 22K gold is widely used for jewellery, although it is softer than lower-karat gold alloys.

Does the calculator include making charges?

The GoldCalc India main calculator can account for making charges, wastage and GST when those values are entered.

Is the calculated price the final jewellery price?

No. The result is an estimate. The final price can vary based on the jeweller, market rate, making charges, wastage and applicable taxes.
